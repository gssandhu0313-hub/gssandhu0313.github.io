The Story

I lead with warmth, show up consistently, and make sure nobody gets left on the outside of the circle, in any language.

From guiding fifth graders through a snowy week at camp to running weekly Scout meetings and selling handmade crochet plushies to a bustling library crowd, I've learned that great leadership is mostly listening, patience, and a little bit of showmanship.

Then I took it global: I studied abroad in Kyoto at Doshisha University, learning to communicate, collaborate and build real friendships across languages and cultures.

Now I'm studying Managerial Business & Economics at UC Merced, ready to bring that people-first, world-ready energy into a related career.
